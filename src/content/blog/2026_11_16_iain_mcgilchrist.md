Some notes on viewing videos with [Dr Iain McGilchrist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iain_McGilchrist)

## The Attack on Life and Understanding Our Times

A [recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNB_1ML_MVE) of a conversation with Mark Vernon.

Dr Gilchrist described the two brain hemispheres and how the left hemisphere prioritizes command and control.  Dr Gilchrist sees current society as dominated by left hemisphere thinking, leading to conflict and environmental destruction.

In contrast the right hemisphere provides creative thinking and more considered problem solving.  Dr Gilchrist suggests that we need a society and an education system that encourages and develops right brain thinking if we are to solve the world's problems.

Dr Gilchrist also talked about the need to think about our personal values and lead lives that fully align with our values.  He sees this as fundamental and that if increasing numbers of people followed this approach we would see real change in the world.

He makes the point that throughout history there have been major problems in society and people have realized that large-scale change can take time to achieve.  We can't expect to all make big changes, but together we can work together to bring about improvements.

Dr Gilchrist recommended a [book](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/66054459-at-work-in-the-ruins) by Dougald Hine: At Work in the Ruins: Finding Our Place in the Time of Climate Crises and Other Emergencies.
