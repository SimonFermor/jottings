I've recently come across a suggestion for help in falling asleep called the cognitive shuffle.  The technique is to think of a word and then think of as many words for each letter in this word.  A refinement is called serial diverse imagining where for each word one imagines onself in different locations and interacting with the objects imagined.

This technique has been developed by Luc P Beaudoin and is described in his [post](https://luccogzest.substack.com/p/how-does-the-mindbrain-fall-asleep) on SubStack.
