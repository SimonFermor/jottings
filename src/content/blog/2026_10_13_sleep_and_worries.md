## What is worry management?

- think about worries differently
- helps deal with anxiety and rumination

Worries can activate flight or fight response, which increases heart rate, sweating etc.

Worries about sleep can make it harder to sleep!

Write down worrisome thoughts, for each thought:

1. What is a less bad way of looking at this?
2. What is the actual likelihood of this happening?
3. What evidence do I have for these worries?
4. What evidence do I have against these worries?
5. How have I coped with similar situations in the past?

When to practice worry management

- at least an hour before bed

Suggestion: set aside time in the day for worries, at least an hour before bedtime
