Interesting [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch) of a talk at Stanford on the changing world of AI, coding and the work environment.

- 5 people you spend your time with have most influence on your habits and outlook
- minimal hiring during the pandemic lead to larges-cale hiring in the years afterwards
- some were hired into the wrong role or not suitable, now being let go (in addition to the impact of AI)
- software engineers are able to be more productive but AI has not had the same impact on product management
- historically 1 to 8 or 10 Product Manager to Developer ratio, now ratio is levelling out and need for more product managers
- conventional advice in interviews is to be firm when answering difficult questions / discussion where you don't have an answer
- important to not come across as aggressive
