Vitamin K is needed for regulating blood clotting, keeping strong bones and preventing calcium buildup in blood vessels.

According to the Cleveland Clinic recommended daily allowances are as follows:

- 90 micrograms for women
- 120 micrograms for men

It appears that vitamin K in supplements is not very effective.  Vitamin K is stored in the body so it's not necessary to get vitamin K every day. I'm aiming to get enough to last the week by eating high vitamin K food at least twice a week.
