Some useful ideas from Arthur Brooks on aging:

Most people are not afraid of death, they are afraid of losing their identity as they grow older.  Elderly people have time and the opportunity to think more about their identity and find do what is most important to them.

Facing fears allows living in the now.

The Buddhist Maranasati meditation focuses on mortality and so develops a deeper appreciation of daily life and mindfulness.  A deeper understanding of impermanence reduces fear and leads to greater life satisfaction.
