Some notes, started after reading a New York Times [article](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/10/15/well/purpose-anxiety-psychology.html) on October 21, 2026

Purpose anxiety - common experience when searching for purpose

Finding satisfactory purpose is often through acting to benefit others.

To find your purpose try different activities and then reflect on experiences that felt meaningful.

Be curious and mimick other peoples good behaviours.
