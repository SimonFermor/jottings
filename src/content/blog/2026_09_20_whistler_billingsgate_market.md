We went to the Frick Collection in NYC in September 2026.  What an incredible place!

After visiting the Frick I looked at their art online and saw that they have a print of an etching by Whistler of Billingsgate Market.  This print was not on display but certainly will be one that I would like to see.  A web search shows that there are copies at many galleries and museums around the world.
