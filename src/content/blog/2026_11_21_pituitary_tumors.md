Some pointers on pituitary tumors:

- almost always benign
- autopsy studies show tumors in about 10% of cases
- pituitary adenomas are most common
- some adenomas do not need surgery

Symptoms leading to discovery of pituitary adenomas are: headache, trauma, cerebrovascular symptoms, internal ear dysfunction, cognitive deficits and visual disturbances
