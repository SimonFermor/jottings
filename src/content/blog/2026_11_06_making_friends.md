Tired of small talk? Some good [advice](https://www.alimattu.com/friends-without-small-talk/) from Dr Ali Mattu on making friends without too much chat:

1: Find a social experience where you don't have to socialize.

2: Become a familiar face.

3: Be interested, not interesting.

4: Share something most people don't know about you.

5: Just keep showing up.
