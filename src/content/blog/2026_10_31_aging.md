This morning I was using tapping to relieve tension and anxiety using a [video short](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/vGAwo3qqOlg) from the Tapping Solution.  This is a good video to run on repeat.

I then looked at other YouTube videos from the tapping solution and watched an [interview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9zjB4EtZN7U) with Cheryl Richardson

There were a couple of good tapping exercises and also lots of positive messages about enjoying later years in life:

- How it's important to learn how to say no more often

- Later years are often a time for inner spiritual growth

- Weight training three times a week is essential

- In later years it is rewarding to fin ways to share life lessons

- Continual learning is important
