Adapted from the New York Times [recipe](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1018028-sourdough-no-knead-bread)

* * *

Timing of 30 minutes in the pan and 15 minutes out is for a silicon bread tin. Longer timing will be needed for baking from cold using a dutch oven.

Oven temperature: 450 °F

Tips
----

Nowhere warm? Warm the oven briefly or place a pan of hot water in the oven.

Ingredients
-----------

* Kosher Salt - 1 teaspoon
* Sesame Seeds - 2 tablespoons
* Sourdough Starter - 180gm
* White Bread Flour - 100gm
* Whole Wheat Bread Flour - 375gm

Instructions
------------

1. Mix the flour and salt in a large mixing bowl.
2. In a small mixing bowl stir together the starter and 300gm of lukewarm water.
3. Pour the sourdough mix into a well in the flour and stir together.
4. Fold together by hand to get a scraggy dough.
5. Leave covered with a tea towel, in a warm place to rise for 10 to 24 hours.
6. Lightly form the dough and place in a dutch oven or bread tin.
7. Place in a warm place for 2 hours.
8. Put the tin into a cold oven and turn on the oven.
9. Bake for 30 minutes and then bake outside the tin for 15 minutes.
