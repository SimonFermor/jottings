Good discussion on the BBC Radio 4 [Free Thinking](https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/b0144txn) [program](https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/m0031vp7) on the role of intellectuals in public life.

Around the table were:

- Emile Chabal - author of a biography about Marxist historian Eric Hobsbawm
- Sophie Scott Brown - researcher
- Muriel Zagha - critic and columnist
- David Goodhart - former editor of Prospect magazine
- Pierre d'Alancaisez - curator and critic

I particularly enjoyed the comparison between the views of intellectuals in the UK versus Europe.

Also interesting to hear about [Eric Hobsbawn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eric_Hobsbawm) and how his books were very popular and helped generate public interest in history.
