1. Each person takes 4 pre-cut small blank papers and writes on each the name of a famous person.
2. Papers are folded over and placed in a bowl.
3. Players are divided into two groups.
4. There are three rounds and for each round groups take turns and for each group, players take turns taking names from the bowl and provide hints to their group for each famous person.
5. The player keeps giving hints until someone in the group guesses the correct name.
6. Each round is one minute.
7. For each round names are taken from the bowl:

- Round 1: Describe the famous person using any words apart from the name
- Round 2: Use two hint words for the person
- Round 3: Use mime to hint at the person

8. After the minute is up the number of famous people identified is added to the group score.  Play then moves to the next player in the opposing group.
9. At the end of each round the folded papers are put back into the bowl.
10. If the minute is not used entirely for the last names in the bowl for round 1 or round 2, then the player uses the remaining time in the next round.

A variation on the rules is [described](https://chriscoyier.net/2022/12/27/how-to-play-celebrity/) by Chris Coyier.
