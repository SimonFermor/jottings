## Evolutionary dietary needs

An [article](https://www.newscientist.com/article/2595250-humans-evolved-to-be-surprisingly-fat-no-wonder-we-struggle-with-diet/) by Sophie Berdugo on what we know about how our dietary needs evolved and the implications for what we should eat.  Key points:

- our bodies try to store fat for feeding our large brains and for lasting on a long hunt
- cooking breaks down cell walls and our bodies have adapted and need cooked food (no more raw diets)
- the Mediterranean diet is probably still the best diet

## The dangers of trying to control everything

In modern society we are encouraged to try and control everything in our lives.  This is not possible and we need to accept uncertainty.

David Robson [reviews](https://www.newscientist.com/article/2596746-is-our-addiction-to-controlling-our-lives-making-us-unhappy/) a book by Anna Lembke, Radical Surrender: Letting go in a world addicted to control.

Is our desire to control what can't be controlled counterproductive?
