Listening to the BBC In Our Time [programme](https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/m002v19c) on M.C. Escher brought back happy memories of visiting the [Escher in the Palace museum](https://escherinhetpaleis.nl/en) in the Hague, the Netherlands.

Escher had difficulty in understanding formal mathematics and at the same time some of his art was very
mathematical and showed some patterns that were not fully understood at the time.
