Listening to the BBC In Our Time [programme](https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/m002v19c) on M.C. Escher brought back happy memories of visiting the [Escher in the Palace museum](https://escherinhetpaleis.nl/en) in the Hague, the Netherlands.

A couple of quotes:

_.. as metamorphis shows, he said he was moving from landscapes to mindscapes_

_... and Escher is a beautiful example of the bridges that you can create between the sciences, mathematics, nature, art and music_

Escher had difficulty in understanding formal mathematics and at the same time some of his art was very
mathematical and showed some patterns that were not fully understood in a formal way at the time.

Escher visited [the Alhambra](https://www.alhambradegranada.org/en/) in [Granada, Spain](https://maps.app.goo.gl/hcGqXCMGDDPGroReA) and took inspration from the patterns of tiles and other designs.  I've not been and must go soon!

For more information see the [Escher foundation website](https://mcescher.com/).
