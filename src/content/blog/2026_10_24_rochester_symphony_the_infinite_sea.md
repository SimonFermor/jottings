What a great performance by the [Rochester Symphony Orchestra](https://rochestersymphony.org/) on October 24.

The program was the Infinite Sea and included:

Paul Dukas: Fanfare from La Péri

Short and rousing sounds to herald the start of the season

Sergei Rachmaninoff: Piano Concerto No. 3 in D minor, Op. 30 with guest artist [Brian Woods](https://www.brianwoodspianist.com/)

Certainly a virtuoso performance by Brian Woods on the piano.  We were in seating where we could see Brian's fingers moving.  Very impressive and he played the entire piece from memory.

Claude Debussy: La Mer

A more relaxing piece after the Rachmaninoff and a good choice for the second half of the concert.
