We like to cook a bibimbap at home and I've been looking for the perfect sauce.  My latest attempt is to mix the following ingredients in a bowl:

Garlic - 1 teaspoon, minced
Gochujang - 2 tablespoons
Maple Syrup - 1 tablespoon
Rice Vinegar - 1 teaspoon
Sesame Oil - 1 tablespoon
Sesame Seeds - 1 tablespoon, toasted
Water - 1 tablespoon

Suggestions welcome for improvement or alternatives.
